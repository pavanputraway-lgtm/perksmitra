PerksMitra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Developer: Bharat Digital Labs

PerksMitra do apps hain: PerksMitra (grahak / customer app) aur PerksMitra Business (dukaan ka app — billing, udhaar khata, stock, GST aur grahak ke rewards). Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaunsa data lete hain, kyun lete hain, aur kise dete hain.

1. Data we collect

  • Customer account: naam, phone number, aur ek PIN (jo hashed roop mein store hota hai, kabhi plain text mein nahi). Optional: email, city, date of birth (sirf birthday reward ke liye), bhasha (language).
  • Business account: dukaan ka naam, maalik ka naam, phone number, email, dukaan ki category, password (sirf hashed). Optional: pata, shehar, pincode, GSTIN, logo, staff accounts.
  • Dukaan ka kaam-kaaj (Business app): bills, saamaan aur stock, udhaar khata, kharcha, kharid, aur dukaan ke grahakon ki list (naam, phone) jo dukaan khud daalti hai. Ye data us dukaan ka hai — hum ise sirf app chalane ke liye rakhte hain.
  • Photos (optional): grahak ki parchi ki photo (order ke liye) aur dukaan ki kharid-bill ki photo — sirf jab aap khud photo chunein / khinchein.
  • Verification: sign-up aur password/PIN reset ke liye phone par SMS code (SMS service provider ke zariye) aur email par code / jaankari (email service provider ke zariye).
  • Notifications: device push token (optional) taaki bill, order, rewards ke notification bhej saken.
  • App usage & errors: app ke kaunse screen khule, kaunsa kaam safal / fail hua, app ki galti (crash) ki jaankari, app ka version, aur dukaan app mein phone ki ek random ID (dukaan ke login wale phones ki list ke liye). Isse hum galtiyan theek karte hain aur dukaan ko madad dete hain.
  • Live help (Business app, optional): jab dukaan khud "Join" dabati hai, tabhi hamara sahayak us help ke dauraan dukaan ki app screen dekh sakta hai (password wali screen kabhi nahi dikhti). App ke andar awaaz wali call mein microphone sirf call ke dauraan chalta hai — hum call record nahi karte.
  • Analytics (Business app): PerksMitra Business app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous app events (for example: app opened, sign-up completed, first bill made) and the device advertising ID, so we can see which advertisements bring real shop owners. We do not send your name, phone number, customers or bill amounts to Google.

2. How we use it

Data sirf app chalane ke liye — loyalty account, points/cards/rewards, bill, khata, stock, GST report, orders, notifications, verification, madad (support), galtiyan theek karna, aur ye samajhna ki hamare kaunse ads se asli dukaandaar judte hain.

3. Who we share it with

  • Hum aapka personal data bechte nahi.
  • Sirf un services ke saath jo hamare liye kaam karti hain: server / database (Supabase), SMS code bhejne wali service, email service, push notification service (Expo / Google), aur Google Analytics for Firebase (upar likha hai).
  • Grahak ka bill / points sirf usi dukaan aur us grahak ko dikhte hain. Dukaan chahe to apne CA (accountant) ko kuch din ke liye sirf-padhne ki ijaazat de sakti hai — ye dukaan ki apni marzi hai.
  • App mein koi ads nahi dikhaye jaate. Advertising ID sirf hamare apne ads ka nateeja naapne ke liye hai.
  • Customers se koi payment / card data collect nahi hoti.

4. Security & isolation

Data secure servers (Supabase) par HTTPS ke through store hota hai. Har dukaan ka data alag rehta hai (tenant isolation) — ek dukaan ka data doosri ko nahi dikhta. Password aur PIN sirf hashed roop mein. Photos private storage mein, sirf sahi user ko thodi der ke link se.

5. Data retention & deletion

Data tab tak rakha jaata hai jab tak account chalu hai (bill aur GST record kanoon ke hisaab se zaroori ho sakte hain). Account / data delete karwana ho to pavanputra.way@gmail.com par email karein — hum aapka data delete kar denge.

6. Children

Ye app general audience aur dukaanon ke liye hai, bachon ko target nahi karta.
