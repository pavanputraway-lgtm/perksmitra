PerksMitra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026 · Developer: Bharat Digital Labs

PerksMitra ("app") ek loyalty & rewards platform hai jisse dukaanein apne customers ko points, scratch cards aur rewards de sakti hain. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaunsa data lete hain aur kaise istemaal karte hain.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: naam, phone number, aur ek PIN (jo hashed roop mein store hota hai, kabhi plain text mein nahi).
  • Optional: city, date of birth (sirf birthday reward ke liye), bhasha (language) preference.
  • Business users: shop ka naam/phone, staff accounts, program settings.
  • Notifications: device push token (optional) taaki rewards/updates ke notification bhej saken.

2. How we use it

Data sirf app ki functionality ke liye — aapka loyalty account chalana, points/cards dikhana, rewards dena, aur notifications bhejna. Bas.

3. What we DON'T do

  • Hum aapka personal data bechte nahi, na kisi third party ko sharing/advertising ke liye dete hain.
  • App mein koi ads nahi.
  • Customers se koi payment / card data collect nahi hoti.

4. Security & isolation

Data secure servers (Supabase) par HTTPS ke through store hota hai. Har dukaan ka data row-level security se alag (tenant isolation) — ek shop ka data doosre ko nahi dikhता. App mein sirf public "anon" key hoti hai.

5. Data retention & deletion

Aap apna account/data delete karwana chahein to pavanputra.way@gmail.com par email karein — hum aapka data delete kar denge.

6. Children

Ye app general audience/businesses ke liye hai, bachon ko target nahi karta.
